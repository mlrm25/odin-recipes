Pork Adobo Recipe

A simple and easy to make delicious Pork Adobo you can enjoy with your family

A classic Filipino dish that everyone will love.

It is easy to customize to your taste. Start with these few basic ingredients and add whatever extras you like.

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ lb. pork bellycubed
  • 1 ½ teaspoons whole peppercorn
  • 5 to 6 pieces dried bay leaves
  • 6 to 8 cloves garliccrushed
  • 5 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 3 tablespoons coconut vinegar
  • 1 ½ cup water or beef broth
  • 3 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Salt
  • Boiled eggs

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan.
  2. Sauté garlic until lightly browned.
  3. Toss in peppercorns and bay leaves for 20 seconds.
  4. Add pork belly, stirring until browned while keeping an eye on the garlic.
  5. Pour in soy sauce and broth, cover, and simmer on low for 40 minutes until tender.
  6. Mix in vinegar, bring back to a boil, and cook for 8 minutes.
  7. Season with salt to taste.
  8. Plate up, add boiled eggs if desired, and serve.

Recipe adapted from Pork Adobo Recipe by Vanjo Merano on Panlasang Pinoy
