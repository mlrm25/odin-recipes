Sisig Recipe

A popular Filipino dish served sizzling on a hotplate.

A classic main dish or a favorite appetizer to enjoy when hanging out with friends.

It's packed with flavor and is a dish you will surely enjoy.

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ lbs. Pork belly
  • 3 tablespoons Knorr Liquid Seasoning
  • 1 lb. pig face
  • 4 ounces chicken liver
  • 2 pieces onion
  • ½ cup chicharon
  • 3 tablespoons Lady’s Choice Mayonnaise
  • 2 teaspoons onion powder
  • ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 3 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 2 quarts water

Steps

  1. Boil pork belly and pig face in water for 1 hour on medium heat, then drain and let cool.
  2. Grill the boiled meats for 5 minutes per side, then chop or mince them.
  3. Heat oil in a pan and sauté onions for 1 minute.
  4. Add chicken liver, cooking and mashing it until broken down completely.
  5. Mix in the chopped meat and crushed chicharon until well combined.
  6. Season with onion powder, ground black pepper, and Knorr Liquid Seasoning, then stir.
  7. Stir in mayonnaise and cook for 3 more minutes, then set aside.
  8. Heat butter on a metal plate, transfer the sisig onto it until it sizzles, and top with extra chicharon.
  9. Serve hot with calamansi or lime alongside white rice.

Recipe adapted from Sisig Recipe by Vanjo Merano on Panlasang Pinoy
