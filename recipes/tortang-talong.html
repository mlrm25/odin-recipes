Tortang Talong Recipe

A classic Filipino eggplant omelet cooked to golden perfection.

A comforting and budget-friendly comfort food that makes for a hearty breakfast or a great meal companion with white rice.

Smoky, savory, and a dish you will surely love.

Ingredients

  • 4 pieces eggplant
  • 4 pieces eggs
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 4 to 6 tablespoons cooking oil

Steps

  1. Grill the eggplants over an open flame until the skin is fully charred and the flesh is tender.
  2. Let them cool, then carefully peel off the burnt skin while keeping the stems attached.
  3. Lay each eggplant flat and gently press it out into an oval shape using a fork.
  4. Whisk the eggs and salt together in a wide bowl.
  5. Dip each flattened eggplant into the egg mixture to coat both sides thoroughly.
  6. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat, fry the coated eggplant, and spoon a little extra egg on top.
  7. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes until golden, flip, and fry the other side for another 2 to 3 minutes.
  8. Repeat the process for the remaining eggplants, adding oil as needed.
  9. Serve hot with warm rice and banana ketchup, and enjoy!

Recipe adapted from Tortang Talong Recipe by Vanjo Merano on Panlasang Pinoy
